Our Mission

EarthGuard is dedicated to protecting, restoring, and promoting sustainable use of terrestrial ecosystems aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 15 (Life on Land). We work to halt deforestation, combat desertification, reverse land degradation, and stop biodiversity loss. Our mission is to ensure that the world's forests, woodlands, grasslands, and wetlands remain vital sources of biodiversity and ecosystem services. Through education, advocacy, and community action, we strive to create a world where nature and humanity thrive together. Every ecosystem we protect contributes to global climate stability, clean water, productive soil, and the survival of countless species that depend on healthy terrestrial environments.
