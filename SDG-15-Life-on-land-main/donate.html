Why Your Support Matters

Terrestrial ecosystems are under unprecedented threat. Every day, forests disappear, wetlands are drained, and species go extinct. EarthGuard works on the frontlines of conservation, but we cannot do it alone. Your generous donation helps us:

🌲

Protect Forests

Establish and strengthen protected areas that prevent deforestation and preserve biodiversity hotspots through partnerships with indigenous communities and local organizations.

💧

Restore Wetlands

Restore degraded wetlands that filter water, prevent floods, and provide critical habitat for millions of species while maintaining crucial carbon storage.

🤝

Empower Communities

Support indigenous peoples and local communities to sustainably manage ecosystems and improve their livelihoods while preserving traditional knowledge.

🔬

Advance Science

Fund research and monitoring to understand ecosystems better and adapt our conservation strategies based on the latest scientific evidence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes! EarthGuard is a registered nonprofit organization. Donations are tax-deductible in most countries. You will receive a receipt for your records.

Where does my money go?

75% of donations directly fund conservation work: ecosystem protection, restoration projects, and community programs. 25% supports operations, research, and monitoring.

Can I donate monthly?

Absolutely! Monthly giving is incredibly valuable as it helps us plan long-term conservation projects. Set up a recurring donation for automatic support.

Can I donate in other currencies?

Yes! Our payment system supports multiple currencies. Select your currency at checkout.

Is my donation anonymous?

You can choose to remain anonymous or have your name listed among our supporters. We respect your privacy preferences.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit/debit cards, bank transfers, PayPal, and cryptocurrency for maximum convenience and accessibility.
