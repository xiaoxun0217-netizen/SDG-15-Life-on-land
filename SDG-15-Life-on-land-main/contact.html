Frequently Asked Questions

How can I get involved with EarthGuard?

There are many ways to support our mission! You can donate, volunteer for projects, participate in our educational programs, or help us spread awareness about ecosystem conservation. Contact us to learn more about opportunities that match your interests and skills.

Do you accept corporate partnerships?

Yes! We actively seek partnerships with organizations committed to environmental conservation. If your company shares our values and wants to support ecosystem protection, we'd love to discuss collaboration opportunities. Reach out to our partnerships team.

How do I donate?

Donating is simple! Visit our Donate page where you can make a one-time contribution or set up monthly giving. We accept various payment methods and provide tax receipts for all donations. Every contribution directly supports our conservation work.

Can I volunteer for EarthGuard?

Absolutely! We welcome volunteers from all backgrounds. Opportunities range from fieldwork and community engagement to office-based support. Please contact us with your interests and availability, and we'll help match you with suitable projects.

How quickly will I receive a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 2-3 business days. For urgent matters, please include "URGENT" in your subject line. During holidays, response times may be longer.

Do you share personal information?

No. We respect your privacy and never share your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent. All data is protected in accordance with international privacy standards.
