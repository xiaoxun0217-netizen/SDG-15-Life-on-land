Ecosystems We Protect

Discover the diverse terrestrial ecosystems that sustain life on Earth and the critical conservation work we do to protect them.

Our Focus Areas

EarthGuard works to protect and restore four major types of terrestrial ecosystems. Each plays a vital role in maintaining global biodiversity, supporting human livelihoods, and regulating Earth's climate systems.

🌲 Forest Ecosystems

Forests are the lungs of our planet, covering about 31% of global land area. They store carbon, provide habitat for 80% of terrestrial species, and support the livelihoods of 1.6 billion people. However, we lose approximately 10 million hectares of forest annually to deforestation.

80% of terrestrial species
1.6B people depend on forests
10M hectares lost yearly
50% destroyed since 1990

Major Threats

  • Deforestation for agriculture and timber
  • Climate change and extreme weather
  • Illegal logging and poaching
  • Infrastructure development

💧 Wetland Ecosystems

Wetlands are among Earth's most productive ecosystems. Though they cover only 6% of the planet's surface, they provide habitat for 40% of all species and store twice as much carbon as forests. Wetlands filter water, prevent floods, and support fisheries that feed millions.

6% of Earth's surface
40% of all species habitat
2x more carbon than forests
87% lost in the last 300 years

Major Threats

  • Drainage for agriculture
  • Water pollution and contamination
  • Invasive species
  • Climate change and altered water cycles

🌾 Grassland Ecosystems

Grasslands cover over 40% of Earth's land area and support unique biodiversity including herds of grazing animals, grassland birds, and soil microorganisms. They are vital carbon reservoirs and support global food production through livestock grazing and crop cultivation.

40% of Earth's land area
11% of carbon stored in soils
100+ grassland bird species at risk
-49% converted to agriculture

Major Threats

  • Conversion to cropland
  • Overgrazing and improper management
  • Invasive plant species
  • Fragmentation from development

🏜️ Desert & Dryland Ecosystems

Deserts and drylands cover about 41% of Earth's land surface and are home to unique adapted species and indigenous communities. These harsh environments are remarkably biodiverse and store significant carbon in soils. They're increasingly threatened by desertification and climate change.

41% of Earth's land
2B+ people affected by desertification
24B hectares degrading yearly
75% live in countries with GDP <$5000

Major Threats

  • Desertification and land degradation
  • Overgrazing and unsustainable agriculture
  • Water scarcity and drought
  • Climate change intensifying aridity
