🌧️ Rainwater Collector

Help protect our ecosystems by collecting rainwater! Dodge obstacles and rack up points to become an EarthGuard hero!

How to Play

Objective: Collect as many raindrops as possible to conserve water!

  • Move your watering can left and right using arrow keys or mouse movement
  • Collect raindrops (💧) to earn points
  • Avoid bombs (💣) - they damage your collection!
  • Avoid lightning (⚡) - it disrupts your efforts!
  • Survive for as long as you can and reach the highest score!

Rewards: Earn achievements and unlock special rewards based on your score!

💡 Did You Know?

Water Conservation

Rainwater harvesting can reduce freshwater consumption by 30-50% in urban areas. This reduces pressure on groundwater resources and helps restore natural hydrological cycles that terrestrial ecosystems depend on—UN Water Conservation Report 2023.

Ecosystem Impact

Wetlands cover only 6% of Earth's surface but provide habitat for 40% of all species and store twice as much carbon as forests. Protecting water cycles is critical for preventing their further destruction—IUCN Wetlands Assessment.

Climate Connection

Climate change is altering global rainfall patterns by 10-15% in many regions, affecting 2.4 billion people who depend on forests for their livelihoods. Water conservation helps ecosystems adapt—IPCC Climate Report 2023.

SDG 15 Goal

SDG 15 targets halting deforestation, restoring degraded forests, and ensuring sustainable use of terrestrial ecosystems. Integrated water management is essential for achieving all SDG 15 targets—UN Sustainable Development Goals Report.
